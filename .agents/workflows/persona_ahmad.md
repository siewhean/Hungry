# Persona: Ahmad (Budget Daily User)

**Profile:** 25, male, Malaysian working in Singapore on work permit. Grab delivery rider. Lives in Jurong West. Halal-only. Budget: < S$6/meal.

**Pain points:**
- Most apps don't show halal certification clearly
- Can't find cheap hawker options for late-night meals (finishes at 11pm)
- Doesn't trust random reviews — wants to see real photos

## Test session

1. Open Hungry (first use)
2. Onboarding: dietary flag = Halal
3. Search "nasi lemak" or "mee goreng"
4. Filter by price: < S$6
5. Check: are halal badges visible?
6. Navigate to a stall → check operating hours include post-10pm options
7. Try Decide-for-Me for "something cheap and filling for supper"

## Success signals

- Halal badge prominent on dish and stall cards
- Price filter works and S$6 threshold filters correctly
- Late-night stalls (10pm–2am) are correctly identified as open
- Decide-for-Me proposes a halal dish

## Failure signals

- No halal filter in DietaryPreferences
- Price not displayed on dish cards in Search Results
- Stall operating hours not shown
