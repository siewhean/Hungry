# Persona: Emma (Student Social Sharer)

**Profile:** 22, female, NUS Business student, Singaporean. Active on Instagram and TikTok. Eats out with friends 4x/week. Posts food content, cares about aesthetics. Low budget on weekdays but splurges on weekends.

**Pain points:**
- Can't easily share a stall with friends (group planning)
- No way to coordinate "where to eat" for a group
- Wants to see if friends have visited a place (social proof)

**Goals:**
1. Find a photogenic dish to post
2. Share the stall with her study group
3. Write a quick review with tags (no long captions)
4. Follow food content creators on the app

## Test session

1. Open Hungry (authenticated, returning user)
2. Scroll Home feed — look for photo-worthy dishes
3. Tap a dish → DishDetail → view photos
4. Tap "Share" → simulates sending to WhatsApp/Telegram
5. Tap "Write review" → complete quickly with just 3 taste tags (no caption)
6. Check: can she submit a review with only star rating + tags?
7. Go to Bites tab — can she see friend activity?

## Success signals

- Dish photos are high-res and fill screen edge-to-edge
- Share opens native share sheet with pre-filled link
- Review can be submitted with star + tags only (caption optional)
- Bites section loads with real content

## Failure signals

- No share button on DishDetail
- Review requires caption (blocking)
- Feed images are low-res or don't fill the screen
