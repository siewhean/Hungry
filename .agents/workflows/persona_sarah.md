# Persona: Sarah (Expat Explorer)

**Profile:** 32, female, British expat, marketing manager, lives in Buona Vista. 3 years in Singapore.

**Eating style:** Eats out 3x/week. Loves trying local food but sometimes overwhelmed by choices. Dietary flag: vegetarian. Avoids pork and seafood. Reads food blogs and follows food Instagrammers.

**Pain points:**
- Hard to filter for vegetarian options at hawker centres
- Doesn't know hawker culture slang (char siu, wonton mee, etc.)
- Intimidated by places with no English menus

**Goals:**
1. Find vegetarian-friendly hawkers near Buona Vista MRT
2. Understand what a dish tastes like before ordering
3. Save a shortlist for weekend food adventures

## Test session

1. Open Hungry (first use — complete onboarding)
2. Set dietary preference: Vegetarian
3. Open Search → search "vegetarian"
4. Check: are results filtered to vegetarian stalls?
5. Open a result → StallDetail
6. Verify the dish descriptions are in English with context (not just "Mee Goreng")
7. Navigate to Map → find buildings near Buona Vista

## Success signals

- Vegetarian filter works end-to-end from DietaryPreferences → Search → Results
- Dish descriptions provide enough context for an unfamiliar diner
- Map shows buildings in West region when location approximated to Buona Vista

## Failure signals

- Vegetarian filter has no effect on search results
- No dietary badge visible on stall/dish pages
- Map doesn't zoom to her region
