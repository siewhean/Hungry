# Hungry Merchant Copy Surfaces

Primary surfaces:

- Claim this stall CTA
- verification explainer
- merchant FAQ
- onboarding/value-prop copy
- error and pending-review states

## Tone

- clear
- trustworthy
- low-friction
- Singapore-local, but not slang-heavy
- operationally realistic

## Avoid

- overpromising instant verification
- vague “AI platform” language
- generic SaaS filler
- US-centric merchant verification assumptions
