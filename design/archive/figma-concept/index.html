Hungry / mobile-first hawker discovery

Reworked UI/UX concept for a dish-first food app, grounded in the PRD, flow spec, and frontend rules.

The design keeps the report-first IA of Discover, Search, Reads, and Profile. The landing experience borrows the best social feed behaviors without becoming a creator platform: rich imagery, quick context, local trust, and fast dish decisions.

Discover = landing + map utility Search = dish-first utility Reads = editorial bridge Profile = taste identity
Primary sources

Hungry PRD, App Flow, Frontend Guidelines

Reference inputs

Mobbin, 21st.dev, Map UI Patterns, NNGroup, Eleken, Dribbble

Visual thesis

Warm editorial surfaces, hawker-local trust signals, and image-led appetite cues.

Content plan

Principles, references, foundations, IA, screens, components, and prototype paths.

Interaction thesis

Quick search entry, decisive filters, floor navigation, and review attachment at the dish level.

00 Product Principles

What changed from the current app direction

The reports stay authoritative. The Figma concept intentionally removes the extra Home and Maps tabs from the information architecture and folds their strongest ideas into Discover and the building drill-down journey.

Dish-first clarity

Photography, price, taste cues, and recommendation language consistently outrank generic venue chrome.

Hawker-local trust

Levels, unit numbers, hygiene grade, closure state, and hours are visible before users commit.

Social, not creator-first

Discover feels alive through stories and media rails, but the app still optimizes for eating decisions.

Utility under appetite

Search, filters, Decide For Me, drill-down, and review flows are designed to shorten time-to-choice.

01 Reference Board

References translated into product decisions

Each source here informs structure or interaction behavior, not direct visual copying. The goal is to combine proven patterns with a Hungry-specific local identity.

Mobbin keep

Use proven mobile feed, onboarding, search, profile, and editorial reading rhythms.

21st.dev adapt

Borrow component anatomy for tabs, search bars, sliders, chips, bottom sheets, and CTA clusters.

Map UI Patterns keep

Use a simple floor-selector and “locate me” mental model for the building and level browsing flow.

NNGroup icon guidance adapt

Pair icons with strong labels in dense mobile contexts. The UI should never rely on icon guessing.

Eleken profile patterns keep

Profile emphasizes user identity, saved content, and history before low-value settings clutter.

Eleken slider patterns keep

Taste filters use dense but touch-safe controls with visible value markers and grouped states.

Dribbble map + food composition adapt

Take composition energy only: photo-first cards, layered maps, and concise metadata.

Avoid avoid

Purple gradients, glossy delivery-app clichés, dashboard card mosaics, and exact competitor layouts.

Typography styles

Display / H1 / Body

Nunito for appetite, hierarchy, and clear utility copy.

Price / Unit / Metadata

DM Mono for price strings, unit numbers, and numerical trust details.

Spacing + surface rules

  • 8-point spacing scale from 4 to 64.
  • Card radii 8 / 12 / 18 with pill tags at 999.
  • Soft, warm surfaces instead of hard white app chrome.
  • 44×44 touch minimum for chips, tabs, and CTA controls.

Motion + accessibility

  • Quick 100ms press feedback and 250-300ms panels/transitions.
  • Text summaries for taste charts and obvious active states for tabs.
  • Reduce motion when needed. Imagery must aid the decision, not decorate.

03 IA + Flows

Report-first navigation model

The final concept restores the 4-tab IA from the reports. Discover becomes the landing surface and absorbs the strongest social and map entry ideas from the current app direction.

Discover → Search → Reads → Profile

Guest browse path

Splash → Discover → building drill-down or dish detail → soft gate only when saving or reviewing.

Search-to-dish path

Search → taste filter → results → dish detail → delivery or review entry.

Map/building path

Discover map entry → building → floor selector → stall → dish preview and menu.

Reads bridge

Reads article → featured stall CTA → stall detail, with editorial context still visible.
