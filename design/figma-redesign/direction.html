Hungry / 00 Direction

Replace the oversized concept board with a tighter, image-led mobile product direction.

The reports remain the product source of truth: 4-tab IA, dish-first search, building-level stall browsing, review attached to dish, and a blog bridge back into discovery. The redesign changes presentation, not product logic. Discover becomes the true landing surface by combining social feed energy, quick search, Decide For Me, and full-screen map entry.

Design principles

What the redesign optimizes for

The current board reads too large and too generic. The new direction is more grounded, local, compact, and operational.

1 / Appetite first

Hero media should earn attention immediately.

Large dish and stall visuals lead screens, but operational metadata stays close to the media so the user can decide fast.

2 / Trust signals

Unit number, level, hours, grade, and proximity remain visible.

Hawker discovery fails if users cannot understand where a stall is or whether it is open.

3 / Social but disciplined

Feed interactions support discovery, not vanity.

Reaction counts and comments make the product feel current, but the CTA always bends back toward a dish or stall detail.

4 / Smaller scale

Typography and shapes now feel like a shipping app.

Display 28–32, headings 20–24, body 13–15, metadata 11–12. Chips and cards are tighter throughout.

Reference strip

How the external sources shape the solution

These are abstracted pattern inputs, not visual templates.

keep Mobbin

Use its strongest mobile feed, onboarding, review, and profile patterns to keep the app visually current and fast to parse.

adapt 21st.dev

Borrow component-level structure for chips, bottom sheets, search bars, and rails, but keep Hungry’s warm local tone instead of generic web polish.

keep Map UI Patterns

Use a full-screen spatial browse with context synchronized into a bottom sheet and a clean indoor selector once a building is chosen.

adapt NNGroup + Eleken

Favor clear icon labeling, restrained mobile imagery, cleaner profile hierarchy, and slider ergonomics that do not overwhelm the dish search flow.

Primary flows

Prototype routes that matter most

Flow 1

Splash → Taste quiz → Discover

First-time entry reaches a live discovery surface quickly instead of dropping into a static utility state.

Flow 2

Discover post → Stall detail → Dish detail

Social content resolves into concrete decisions instead of endless feed consumption.

Flow 3

Discover → Map → Building → Floor → Stall

Spatial discovery makes hawker centres legible and reduces the “black box” problem identified in the PRD.

Flow 4

Search → Filter → Results → Dish detail

Search remains dish-first and compact, with the sliders acting as a real ranking tool.
