Hungry v2 / 05 Quick Review Flow

30-second review — tap photo, rate, tag, done.

The review flow is designed to be completable in under 30 seconds with no mandatory text input. 4 steps: dish photo confirmation → 5-star rating → taste tag grid (tap, don't type) → optional 140-char caption. Each step is one screenful. The review attaches to the dish, not just the stall. Gamification: points earned shown on success screen to build habit loops.

Design Notes

Why this review UX works for hawker discovery

Design decision

No mandatory text — tags do the work

Writing a review is the single biggest friction point. The 12 taste tags cover 90% of what users want to say. Text becomes "add anything else" — optional, never required. This dramatically increases review volume from casual users.

Design decision

Review attaches to the dish, not the stall

Hawker stalls sell 2–5 dishes. Attaching the review to a specific dish (not just "the stall") creates the dish-level intelligence that powers Decide for Me recommendations. The taste profile radar on stall pages is built from these tags.

Design decision

Progress bar + step dots: users know they're almost done

Showing the 4-step progress bar prevents abandonment. Users who can see "75% done" are far more likely to complete than users who don't know how many steps remain. The bar turns fully green on success.

Design decision

Gamification: points → Hawker level badges

The success screen shows points earned and progress to next level (Hawker Expert, etc.). This mirrors Burpple's ranked system — users feel recognised for contributing. Badges appear on Profile and can be shared. Review habit forms faster.
