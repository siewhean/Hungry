# Persona Audit Feedback: Batch 2 (April 11)

This document records the feedback and issues identified by our specialized agents during the final design sprint.

## Persona Set A: Discovery & Efficiency

### Persona D: The Solo Business Traveler
*   **Issue:** Information Gap on environment. Needs to know "busy-ness" and solo-seating availability.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **"Live Crowd"** indicators across all map and list views.

### Persona E: The Budget-Conscious Family Head
*   **Issue:** Lack of "Group Context" (e.g., is it a large hawker center or a tiny shop?).
*   **Fix:** Standardized building-level metadata prominence on Stall Detail pages.

### Persona F: The Food Content Creator
*   **Issue:** Progress fragility during posting. Fear of losing caption drafts.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **"Unfinished Posts"** recovery section on the Profile home screen.

## Persona Set B: Late Night & Safety

### Persona G: The Midnight Craver
*   **Issue:** "Open Now" filters aren't sticky enough.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **Sticky Map Filters** that persist across app sessions.

### Persona H: The Corporate Event Planner
*   **Issue:** Nominations in the Wheel can be biased if one person adds too many items.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **Wheel Entry Limits** (Host controls for 3, 5, or Unlimited).

### Persona I: The Dietary-Restricted Tourist
*   **Issue:** Needs proactive safety checks for group nominations.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **Dietary Warning Alerts** and "Group Needs" header in the Wheel.

## Persona Set C: Niche Safety & Luxury

### Persona J: The Dietary-Strict Vegan
*   **Issue:** Broad "Crave" categories make it hard to find safe nominations.
*   **Fix:** Implemented **Taste-Prioritized Sorting** and "MATCH" badges in the wheel nomination sheet.

### Persona K: The Impulsive High-Earner
*   **Issue:** Wants to skip "Busy" places immediately.
*   **Fix:** Elevated the **"Live Crowd" badge** to be a primary visual anchor on the map carousel.

### Persona L: The Regional Tourist
*   **Issue:** Needs deep-links to external navigation apps.
*   **Resolution:** Planned for V2 (Ready for implementation).
