# @hungry_singapore Brand Kit

## Position

Hungry Singapore is a dish-first food discovery page for people deciding what to eat now. It should feel like a sharp local food friend: useful, warm, craving-led, and easy to share.

Core promise: Find the dish. Not just the restaurant.

## Audience

- Singapore locals, office workers, students, foodies, expats, and tourists.
- People asking "Eat what ah?", "Where got good?", "What is nearby?", or "What should I order?"
- People who save food posts as future lunch, dinner, supper, or weekend plans.

## Voice

- Local, direct, casual, and craving-led.
- Short lines. Simple words. Natural Singapore rhythm.
- Use phrases like "Eat what ah?", "Save this for lunch break", "shiok", "makan", and "settle already" when they fit naturally.
- Avoid corporate launch copy, fake hype, and overclaiming.

## Logo Usage

- Use `hungry_logo_icon_transparent.png` as the small corner stamp on carousel slides.
- Use `hungry_logo_text_transparent.png` on final slides, profile assets, highlight covers, and brand boards.
- Keep the logo on clean warm cream, white, or navy.
- Do not distort, outline, shadow, recolor, or place the logo on noisy food areas without a clean backing shape.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hungry Orange | `#E8531E` | Logo, key stamps, CTAs |
| Burnt Chili | `#C43E10` | Pressed/darker accents |
| Warm Cream | `#FFF7ED` | Main editorial background |
| Navy | `#1A2E44` | Strong contrast panels and headings |
| Charcoal | `#111827` | Primary copy |
| Hawker Amber | `#F5A623` | Ratings, price highlights, warmth |
| Teal Tag | `#14B8A6` | Dietary/location accents outside this no-chip carousel |

## Type

- Headline: Fraunces Black or a rounded editorial serif.
- Body: Nunito or Nunito Sans.
- Numeric or small editorial accents: DM Mono.
- Rule: max two visible font families per carousel, with DM Mono only for restrained editorial text.

## Graphic System

- Use editorial rules, clean image crops, restrained labels, simple map lines, star ratings, and save/share prompts.
- Avoid chip-style UI, pill tags, badges, progress dots, page numbers, and carousel-count indicators inside the artwork.
- Keep details useful. Do not add decorative stickers that do not help the reader decide.

## Photography

- Food-first, close, warm, and appetising.
- Prefer top-down or 45-degree dish shots.
- Show sauce gloss, steam, chopsticks/spoons, hawker trays, kopi cups, and tissue packets.
- Use Singapore cues: hawker tables, lunch crowds, MRT/lunch-break framing, and real dish names.

## Layout Rules

- Export size: 1080 x 1350.
- One main idea per slide.
- Big headline at top, food as hero, proof or CTA at bottom.
- Headline target: under 12 words.
- Body target: under 24 words.
- Do not use page numbers, progress dots, or navigational indicators inside the post artwork.

## Do

- Lead with cravings.
- Make posts saveable with lists, maps, prices, situations, or decision shortcuts.
- Use local phrases naturally.
- Repeat the visual system: orange accent, cream base, navy text, food-first images.

## Do Not

- Do not become a generic review page.
- Do not claim "best in Singapore" unless sourced.
- Do not crowd slides with paragraphs.
- Do not make every slide orange; let food imagery carry appetite.
