## Posting Style

- Format: 1080 x 1350 Instagram carousel.
- First post role: launch a repeatable "Eat What Ah?" series.
- Visual flow: hook -> relatability -> cravings -> value -> dish-first angle -> save/share CTA.
- Artwork rule: no chips, page numbers, progress dots, or navigation indicators inside the post.
- Same-day Story: poll followers with `Comfort`, `Spicy`, `Budget`, `Supper`.

## Posting Time

- Weekdays: 11:15am-12:15pm or 5:30pm-6:45pm.
- Weekend: 10:30am-11:30am.

## Engagement

- Pin this comment: `Drop your craving + area. I will suggest a dish.`
- Reply to comments in the first hour.
- Turn recurring comment patterns into the next carousel.
