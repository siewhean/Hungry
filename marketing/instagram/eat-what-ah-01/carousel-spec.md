# Eat What Ah? Carousel Spec

## Concept

Title: Eat What Ah?

Hook: Still asking "eat what ah?" Save this.

Theme: A Singapore craving-based decision guide. Instead of starting with restaurants, the post starts with the feeling or eating occasion and points people to dishes.

Marketing psychology:

- Curiosity gap: Which craving are you today?
- Save trigger: practical lunch/dinner decision shortcut.
- FOMO: do not waste a good meal slot.
- Social proof: ask followers to comment their go-to dish.
- AIDA: hook, interest, craving, action.
- Clear flow: hook, relatability, craving options, budget value, dish-first positioning, action.

Visual constraints:

- No chips, pill labels, price badges, progress dots, arrows, page numbers, or slide-count indicators inside the artwork.
- No visible carousel numbering such as `01/07` or `7 cravings`.
- Use professional food imagery, editorial crops, and clean typography instead of app-like UI elements.

| Close | Final CTA | Next time: do not ask "eat what ah?" | Save this. Send to the friend who always cannot decide. | Comment: comfort / spicy / budget / supper. |
