# Hungry Creator, Press, And Hawker Outreach Playbook

## Objective

Turn the Hungry launch story into something easy for creators, community admins, local press, and hawker operators to understand and share.

## Segment Map

| Segment | Why they matter | Angle | Asset to send | Ask |
|--------|-----------------|-------|---------------|-----|
| Singapore food micro-creators | They translate product utility into trusted local taste language | "Dish-first discovery for daily food decisions" | 1-page preview deck + short demo clip | Share, react, or preview |
| Food photographers and local visual storytellers | They can make the product feel local and appetizing without hype | "A better UX for local food discovery" | Mood board + UI stills | Collaborate on launch assets |
| Hawker bloggers and food writers | They already explain stalls and dishes to the market | "From editorial inspiration to actionable discovery" | Waitlist page + Reads bridge concept | Cover the angle or introduce feedback |
| Reddit / Telegram / Facebook community admins | They can seed the right conversations | "Utility-first local product, not spam" | Plain-language post draft + one mockup | Allow a useful community post |
| Local press / startup / culture desks | They can frame the story beyond social content | "Why Singapore food discovery still starts with the wrong object" | Short pitch note + screenshots | Cover or interview |
| Hawker stall owners | They need a clear value prop with low friction | "Get discovered by dish" | Simple one-pager + QR concept | Register interest |

## Creator Outreach Angle

### What to lead with
- This is not another restaurant directory
- The product solves a daily local problem: deciding what to eat
- Hawker-centre discovery is a real UX problem, not just a content problem

### What to send
- 1 short intro paragraph
- 1 vertical demo clip
- 3 clean screenshots
- 1 simple ask

### Suggested ask
- "Would you be open to reacting to this concept?"
- "Would you share feedback on whether this feels useful?"
- "If you like the angle, would you post or include it in a local food roundup?"

## Press Angle Options

- A dish-first alternative to restaurant-first local discovery
- Why hawker centres still feel digitally underserved
- How taste-based discovery could change everyday food choice in Singapore
- Why local-food content should lead to action, not just inspiration

## Creator DM Template

Hi [Name] - we are building Hungry, a dish-first food discovery app for Singapore.

The idea is simple: most people know the dish they want before they know the venue, but most apps still force them to browse restaurants first. Hungry flips that around with dish-first search, nearby discovery, and hawker-centre context.

I thought this might fit your audience because you already help people figure out what is worth eating locally. If you are open to it, I can send a short preview and a few screenshots.

## Press Pitch Template

Subject: Story idea - a Singapore food app that starts with the dish, not the restaurant

Hi [Name],

We are building Hungry, a Singapore dish-first food discovery app focused on hawker centres, food courts, and nearby local cravings.

The story is straightforward: most food discovery tools still start with the venue even when the user starts with a dish. Hungry is designed to solve that through dish-first search, taste-based discovery, and clearer hawker-centre context.

If that sounds relevant for your audience, I can send a tighter summary, screenshots, and the waitlist page.

## Community Admin Template

Hi [Admin name] - we are working on a local product concept called Hungry.

It is a dish-first food discovery app for Singapore, built around one practical problem: people usually know what they want to eat before they know which venue to open.

I have a short, non-spammy draft post plus one mockup if you are open to a community share. Happy to tailor it to your group rules.

## Hawker Outreach Script

Hi, we are building Hungry, a food discovery app that helps people find stalls by the dish they want to eat.

A lot of discovery apps still start from the venue, which makes smaller stalls harder to find unless people already know the name. We want to make it easier for people nearby to discover the dishes a stall actually serves.

If that sounds relevant, we can add you to the hawker-interest list and share updates when the visibility tools are ready.

## Hawker One-Pager Outline

**Headline:** Get discovered by dish

**Sections:**
- The problem: people search for food differently than platforms are built
- What Hungry is trying to fix
- What stall information matters: dish name, floor, unit, hours, delivery links
- What future merchant-side value could look like
- How to register interest

## QR And Field-Test Concept

### Counter card concept
- A6 card or table card
- Front: "Looking for what to eat nearby? Join the Hungry waitlist."
- Back: hawker-side message, "Run a stall? Register your interest."

### Use case
- Hawker-centre walk-throughs
- Creator meetups
- Food pop-ups
- Small field interviews

## Follow-Up Cadence

- Day 0: initial note
- Day 3-5: one short follow-up with a tighter angle
- Day 10+: close the loop politely if there is no response

## Outreach Rules

- Keep every outreach message short enough to read on a phone
- Lead with the problem, not the company story
- Make one ask at a time
- Do not mass-DM low-fit accounts
- Do not overstate launch readiness or adoption
