# Hungry Channel Playbook

## Objective

Run a 30-day, organic-first awareness sprint that introduces Hungry to Singapore diners and builds early-access demand through a single destination: the waitlist page.

## Operating Rules

- Organic-first only. No paid-media assumptions in this playbook.
- Every outward-facing asset should drive either awareness, saves/shares, comments, or waitlist clicks.
- The waitlist page is the home base. Social channels exist to feed it.
- The consumer story leads. The hawker story supports credibility and future expansion.

## Core Channel Stack

| Channel | Role | Best format | Cadence | Primary CTA | Success signal |
|--------|------|-------------|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Instagram | Visual discovery and save/share | Reels, carousels, stories | 4-5 touches/week | Waitlist or save/share | Saves, shares, profile visits |
| TikTok / Reels-native video | Fast awareness and hook testing | 15s and 30s short video | 3-4 posts/week | Waitlist | Watch time, comments, shares |
| Xiaohongshu-friendly content | Discovery and local-food aspiration | Image carousel, concise narrative post | 2 posts/week | Waitlist or save | Saves and reposts |
| Reddit / Telegram / Facebook communities | Utility-first discussion and validation | Text post, image post, product mockups | 2-3 community posts/week | Comment or waitlist | Useful discussion, referrals |
| LinkedIn | Founder and product narrative | Build-in-public, product angle, hawker-tech story | 1-2 posts/week | Waitlist or intro | Profile visits, intros |
| Creator outreach | Earned credibility and content distribution | DMs, private preview decks, creator briefs | Ongoing | Trial or share | Responses, creator interest |
| Press and blog outreach | Story amplification | Short pitch, founder note, screenshots | Weekly batch | Cover the story | Replies and mentions |
| On-ground hawker materials | Future field-test bridge | QR cards, one-pager, counter card | Pilot-ready | Hawker interest | QR scans and conversations |

## Content Pillars

### 1. Decision relief
Show the problem people already feel:
- "What should I eat right now?"
- "I know the dish, not the venue."
- "I do not want to spend ten minutes deciding lunch."

### 2. Dish-first versus venue-first
Use strong contrast:
- Search by dish versus search by restaurant
- Craving-led choice versus directory-led browsing
- One confident answer versus endless list fatigue

### 3. Hawker clarity
Show why Hungry matters locally:
- Floor and unit context
- What to order, not just where to go
- Better discovery for iconic dishes and everyday stalls

### 4. Taste-led discovery
Make craving feel concrete:
- spicy, rich, sweet, aromatic
- taste profile as a shortcut to confidence
- Decide For Me as the "I cannot decide" release valve

### 5. Hawker and local culture
Support the product story with local relevance:
- stall spotlight concepts
- hawker operator value story
- why Singapore food discovery deserves a better product surface

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day type | Goal | Typical asset |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Monday | Hook the problem | Reel or short video |
| Tuesday | Teach the product difference | Carousel or side-by-side post |
| Wednesday | Community conversation | Reddit / Telegram / FB post |
| Thursday | Local proof | Hawker spotlight or explainer |
| Friday | Product walkthrough | UI-led video or carousel |
| Weekend | Culture and shareability | Local-food opinion, checklist, or "save this" guide |

## Asset Mix

- Product UI for proof and believability
- Real food and hawker imagery for appetite and local texture
- Motion graphics and AI-assisted transition plates only where they do not create fake product claims

## Organic Growth Mechanics

### Community loop
Post useful prompts in local food communities, gather language from comments, and feed that language back into the next round of copy.

### Creator loop
Offer creators a tight angle, a ready-made content brief, and early access or a preview package. Reduce work for them.

### Waitlist loop
Every high-performing post should be repurposed into:
- one waitlist page block
- one email or DM follow-up
- one pinned or evergreen profile asset

## Prioritization

If resources are thin, do these first:
1. Instagram / short-form video
2. Waitlist page
3. Community posts
4. Creator outreach
5. LinkedIn founder narrative

## What Not To Do

- Do not launch with broad paid traffic before the waitlist destination is clear.
- Do not pretend the app is already publicly available.
- Do not use fake adoption numbers or "featured in" badges.
- Do not make Hungry sound like a generic restaurant app or lifestyle media brand.
