# Hungry Positioning And Messaging

## Positioning Core

**Primary line:** Find the dish. Not just the restaurant.

**Supporting line:** Dish-first food discovery for Singapore. Makan nearby with more confidence.

**Category:** Consumer mobile food discovery

**What makes Hungry different:** Hungry starts with what people actually want to eat, then helps them choose the right stall through taste fit, nearby relevance, and hawker-centre clarity.

## Audience Priority

### Primary audience: Singapore diners
- Office workers deciding lunch quickly
- Nearby diners choosing a hawker-centre or food-court meal
- Tourists and expats who want local food without the usual confusion

### Secondary audience: Hawker stall owners
- Operators who want younger or newer customers to discover them
- Stalls that benefit when people search by dish instead of by venue brand

## Messaging Tracks

### Consumer hero narrative
**Problem:** Most food apps make people browse the wrong thing first. When people are hungry, they want the dish, not a directory of venues.

**Promise:** Hungry gets people from craving to confident choice faster.

**Proof points to repeat:**
- Dish-first search
- Six-axis taste filtering
- Decide For Me for quick nearby recommendations
- Floor and unit context for hawker centres and food courts
- Reviews and delivery-link context where available

**Core payoff:** Less time deciding. Better odds of picking the right dish the first time.

### Hawker secondary narrative
**Problem:** Hawker stalls often depend on foot traffic, regulars, and scattered social content, even when people nearby are actively craving what they sell.

**Promise:** Hungry gives dishes, stalls, and local context a clearer path to discovery.

**Proof points to repeat:**
- Dish-first search surfaces the specific dish, not just the venue
- Stall metadata includes floor, unit, hours, hygiene grade, and delivery-link support
- Future merchant story already exists in the roadmap and product context

**Core payoff:** Better discoverability without turning the stall into a full-time content machine.

## Approved Claims We Can Make Now

These are grounded in repo documentation and current product artifacts.

- Hungry is a dish-first food discovery app for Singapore.
- The product is built around hawker centres, food courts, and local dishes.
- The experience includes dish-first search, taste-based filtering, nearby discovery, stall detail, reviews, and delivery links.
- The product is designed to help people decide what to eat faster.
- Hungry has a hawker-centre story, not just a restaurant story.

## Claims That Must Be Framed Carefully

- "AI" should only be used for future roadmap references such as taste tagging or recommendation work.
- Merchant analytics, vendor dashboards, and digital ordering must be labeled as future or roadmap work.
- Do not imply live launch scale, customer adoption, or real merchant partnerships that are not documented.

## Messaging Guardrails

- Lead with decision relief, not abstract brand language.
- Use local clarity, not tourism cliches.
- Be warm and useful, never startup-generic.
- Avoid fake scarcity, fake testimonials, fake metrics, or fake partner logos.
- Keep one CTA per asset whenever possible.

## Waitlist CTA Language

Preferred CTA options:
- Join the waitlist
- Get early access
- Be first to try Hungry

Avoid:
- Book a demo
- Download now
- Start free trial
- Claim your growth edge

## Creator And Community Language

Use these outreach frames:
- "We are building a dish-first way to discover what to eat in Singapore."
- "We want to make hawker-centre discovery more useful, not more gimmicky."
- "The goal is practical: less time deciding, more confidence ordering."
