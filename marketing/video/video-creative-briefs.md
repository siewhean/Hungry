# Hungry Video Creative Briefs

## Production Rule

Use actual app captures and real food / hawker visuals as the proof layer whenever possible. AI-generated video should support transitions, motion backgrounds, or generic atmosphere only. Do not imply a real stall partnership or real dish availability unless the source material is genuine.

## Video 1: 15-Second Social Hook

**Objective:** Stop the scroll and introduce the dish-first idea in one clean contrast.

**Audience:** Singapore diners who routinely spend too long deciding lunch or dinner.

**Platform:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds

**Core angle:** "You know the dish. Most apps still make you search by venue."

**Storyboard:**
- 0-3s: Fast frustration hook. Endless restaurant list scroll, confused face, or bold kinetic text.
- 3-6s: Hard contrast. "Search by restaurant" versus "Search by laksa."
- 6-11s: Show Hungry UI: dish search, taste fit, nearby options.
- 11-15s: End on product mark, local dish imagery, and waitlist CTA.

**Caption overlays:**
- "What should I eat right now?"
- "Most apps start with the wrong search"
- "Hungry starts with the dish"
- "Join the waitlist"

**Voiceover script:**
You know the dish. You just do not know the stall.

Hungry helps you search by craving, compare nearby options, and decide faster.

Join the waitlist.

**Silent-viewing plan:** Every beat must be understandable with captions alone.

**Asset mix:** 60% app UI, 40% editorial food and hawker texture

**Hook variants:**
- "Still taking 10 minutes to decide lunch?"
- "Search laksa, not just locations."
- "Not another restaurant app."

## Video 2: 30-Second Product Explainer

**Objective:** Explain how Hungry works in a realistic hawker-centre lunch scenario.

**Audience:** Interested diners who need one layer deeper than the hook.

**Platform:** Instagram, TikTok, landing page, creator reposts

**Format:** 9:16 and 16:9 cutdowns, 30 seconds

**Core angle:** "From craving to stall in a faster, clearer flow."

**Storyboard:**
- 0-3s: "What should I eat right now?" hook
- 3-8s: Show why venue-first browsing is slow
- 8-15s: Introduce dish-first search and taste filters
- 15-22s: Show nearby results, ratings, and stall context
- 22-27s: Show stall detail, floor and unit, and review cues
- 27-30s: Join the waitlist

**Caption overlays:**
- "Start with the dish"
- "Filter by taste"
- "Compare nearby"
- "Find the right stall"
- "Join the waitlist"

**Voiceover script:**
Choosing what to eat should not take longer than eating it.

Hungry is a dish-first food discovery app for Singapore. Search for what you are craving, filter by taste, compare nearby options, and head straight to the right stall.

Join the waitlist to try it first.

**Silent-viewing plan:** Use larger headline captions on the first two beats, then smaller UI-support captions in the middle section.

**Asset mix:** 50% app UI, 50% editorial food and hawker environment

**Edit notes:**
- Keep transitions clean, not flashy
- Use warm palette accents from the product
- Make the last frame readable without audio

## Video 3: Hawker-Side Mini Story

**Objective:** Show why Hungry matters for stalls as well as diners without making the campaign feel B2B-first.

**Audience:** Hawker stall owners, local press, creators, and diners who care about local-food culture.

**Platform:** LinkedIn, Instagram, outreach decks, creator packages

**Format:** 20-30 seconds, 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns

**Core angle:** "People search for the dish. Stalls should be discoverable that way."

**Storyboard:**
- 0-4s: Hawker prep and stall texture
- 4-8s: "Great dishes still get buried when discovery starts at the venue"
- 8-16s: Show Hungry dish search leading to stall context
- 16-24s: Reinforce visibility, floor/unit clarity, and delivery-link support
- 24-30s: CTA for hawker interest or general waitlist

**Caption overlays:**
- "Great dishes still get buried"
- "People search for the dish"
- "Hungry helps them find the stall behind it"
- "Register hawker interest"

**Voiceover script:**
Some of the best local food is still too hard to find.

Hungry is built to help people search by dish first, then discover the stall behind it with better local context.

If you run a stall and want to hear more, register your interest.

**Silent-viewing plan:** Use clear caption framing because this concept will often be watched without audio in feeds.

**Asset mix:** 40% UI, 60% hawker texture and documentary-style footage

**Safety note:** Do not imply an endorsement from a real stall unless the stall has actually agreed to participate.
