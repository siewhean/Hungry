The Dish-First Philosophy

Traditional food apps focus on restaurants. They tell you the ambiance is nice and the service is fast. But what if you just want the best Nasi Lemak within a 5km radius?

We flip the model. Hungry indexes the atomic unit of the culinary world: the dish itself. By organizing our data around individual food items, we surface hidden gems in hawker centres that might otherwise be overshadowed by slick marketing from big restaurant groups.
