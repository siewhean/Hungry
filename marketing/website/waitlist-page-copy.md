## FAQ

**Question:** Is Hungry live yet?
**Answer:** Not publicly. The current focus is building the launch waitlist and shaping early access.

**Question:** Is this only for hawker centres?
**Answer:** Hawker centres and food courts are central to the story, but the broader product scope covers local dining discovery across Singapore.

**Question:** What makes Hungry different from Google Maps or GrabFood?
**Answer:** Hungry starts with the dish, then helps you choose the right stall with more useful local context.

**Answer:** No. The product is utility-first. Reads and content support discovery, but the main job is helping you decide what to eat.
