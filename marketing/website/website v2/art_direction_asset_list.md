# Hungry Art Direction and Asset List

## 1. Asset Strategy Overview

The Hungry website redesign should use a controlled mix of:

- food photography
- Singapore local food atmosphere
- app UI mockups
- merchant category imagery
- subtle hawker-inspired visual elements

The goal is to make the website feel local, premium, immersive, and appetising without making it visually cluttered or touristy.

## 2. Visual Direction Summary

### Final visual direction

```text
Premium Singapore food discovery experience
with subtle hawker culture cues,
balanced across hawkers, restaurants, cafés,
and home-based food businesses.
```

### Visual emphasis

Use a balanced mix of:

1. food photography
2. app UI
3. Singapore local atmosphere

### Mood

- warm
- premium
- local
- editorial
- immersive
- trustworthy
- appetising

## 3. Core Visual References

The design should draw inspiration from:

- Singapore hawker signage
- kopitiam / hawker atmosphere
- local dish visuals
- menu boards
- food photography
- modern consumer app websites
- premium editorial food guides

Avoid:

- generic delivery app visuals
- overused tourism clichés
- cartoon-heavy design
- low-quality food images
- cluttered collage design
- fake or unrealistic food imagery

# 7. Image Usage Rules

## Do

- use consistent warm lighting
- keep images realistic and appetising
- match the Hungry palette
- crop images intentionally
- optimise images for web
- use responsive image sizes
- use alt text
- maintain balance between food, app UI, and atmosphere

## Do not

- use fake logos or visible copyrighted brands
- use watermarked images
- use low-resolution images
- show unrealistic or distorted food
- rely only on hawker imagery
- make the site feel touristy
- use identifiable people without permission

# 10. Asset QA Checklist

Before using any asset, check:

- does it feel local to Singapore?
- does it match the Hungry palette?
- does it feel premium?
- does it avoid generic delivery-app vibes?
- is it high resolution?
- is it optimised for web?
- does it support the section’s message?
- does it avoid fake claims or fake testimonials?
- does it include alt text?
- is it legally safe to use?

The visual world of Hungry should feel like:
