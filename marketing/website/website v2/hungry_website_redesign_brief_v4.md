# Hungry Website Redesign Brief — Finalised Direction v4

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document updates the Hungry website brief with the confirmed redesign direction.

The goal is to redesign the website into an **immersive, interactive, motion-led product website** that blends Hungry’s existing brand system with the warmth and familiarity of **Singapore hawker culture**, while also representing **restaurants, cafés, and home-based food businesses**.

This is not meant to be a basic landing page.
It should feel like a **premium product launch experience** with strong storytelling, high visual polish, and meaningful interaction.

---

## 2. Final Experience Vision

### Experience summary
Hungry’s website should feel like:

- an immersive scroll story
- a premium product launch website
- a local Singapore food experience
- a motion-led app showcase
- a conversion-focused platform for both foodies and merchants

### One-line experience goal

> Design a highly immersive, premium, motion-led website for Hungry that brings together food photography, app UI, and Singapore local food atmosphere — with strong hawker culture cues and a clear merchant-first discovery value proposition.

## 4. Positioning

### Product positioning
Hungry is a Singapore food discovery app that helps:

- **foodies** discover dishes, reviews, rankings, menus, and stall information across Singapore
- **merchants** get discovered by hungry nearby users and present their business clearly

### Core positioning line

> Hungry helps people discover food around them and helps merchants get discovered by nearby hungry customers.

### Brand idea from existing design system

> Find the dish. Not just the restaurant.

This should remain central to the redesigned website.

---

## 5. Redesign Goals

The redesigned site should:

1. make Hungry feel like a real, polished, exciting product
2. differentiate Hungry from generic food apps and delivery apps
3. create a strong emotional connection through local cultural cues
4. visually showcase the future app experience
5. convince foodies to join the waitlist
6. convince merchants to register interest
7. make merchants feel that Hungry can help them **get discovered**
8. remain premium, performant, and mobile-first

---

## 6. Brand and Emotional Direction

### Emotional tone
Hungry should feel:

- warm
- local
- premium
- immersive
- useful
- trustworthy
- slightly playful
- community-driven

### What the user should feel
When someone visits the site, they should feel:

- “This feels built for Singapore.”
- “This is not just another food app.”
- “The app looks polished and useful.”
- “I want to try this.”
- “If I own a food business, I want people to discover me here.”

### What the site should not feel like
Avoid:

- generic startup template
- flashy animation demo with no purpose
- food delivery clone
- overly western restaurant-brand feel
- childish or cartoon-like design
- heritage poster aesthetic without product clarity

---

## 7. Visual Direction — Local Food Culture, Premium Execution

### Theme statement

> Warm Singapore food culture, expressed through a premium and immersive digital interface.

### Main visual ingredients
The site should visually blend:

- premium app launch design
- hawker signage inspiration
- realistic dish imagery
- local food atmosphere
- polished app UI
- editorial storytelling structure

### Business categories that should be visually represented
The site should not visually represent only hawker stalls.
It should include and signal support for:

- hawker stalls
- kopitiam stalls
- food courts
- cafés
- restaurants
- home-based food businesses

### Recommended cultural cues
Use these cues thoughtfully:

- subtle hawker signboard references
- stall/menu board framing ideas
- warm local food environment photography
- dish callout cards
- neighbourhood discovery cues
- local food textures and atmosphere
- selective Singapore-local phrases such as “makan”, “hawker centre”, “nearby”, “open now”

### Avoid overdoing these
Do not:

- use Merlion / skyline clichés heavily
- make the site feel like a tourism campaign
- overwhelm layouts with too many cultural decorations
- over-style the site to the point where the product becomes unclear

### Colour usage philosophy
The site should feel mostly warm and refined.
Recommended ratio:

- 55% warm backgrounds / white / cream
- 25% deep navy and neutral structure
- 10% orange accents and CTA focus
- 5% amber for highlights and food cues
- 5% green / red for status cues

### Colour behaviour
- Use **Hungry Orange** for primary CTA only.
- Use **Deep Navy** for headings and premium contrast.
- Use **Amber** sparingly for ranking, “must try”, or food emphasis.
- Avoid random gradients and unrelated bright colours.

## 10. Motion and Interaction Strategy

The redesign should be **highly immersive**, but motion must remain purposeful.

### Motion principles
Animations should:

- guide attention
- reveal story progressively
- make the product feel alive
- support app understanding
- create delight without hurting usability

### Motion intensity
- high
- cinematic in feel, but still polished and controlled

### Priority motion effects
The following are confirmed priorities:

#### 1. Interactive cards
Use for:
- food categories
- dish recommendations
- foodie vs merchant cards
- merchant value cards
- reviews / ranking cards

Possible behaviours:
- hover expansion
- tilt / depth response
- tap-to-expand on mobile
- animated overlays

#### 2. Horizontal carousel
Use for:
- featured dishes
- app screens
- local business categories
- review / ranking stories
- Singapore food journey sections

Possible behaviours:
- drag/swipe enabled
- scroll-linked motion
- snap interactions
- layered cards entering/exiting smoothly

#### 3. Reveal-on-scroll
Use for:
- storytelling progression
- section introductions
- sticky phone feature reveals
- merchant workflow explanation
- local culture visuals

Possible behaviours:
- fade + slide
- masked reveal
- image reveal
- staggered card entrance
- content pinning with progressive transitions

### Additional supporting motion (optional but aligned)
These are not mandatory, but may be used if they strengthen the experience:

- subtle parallax layers in the hero
- sticky story sections
- animated app UI transitions in a device frame
- background texture movement
- controlled hover states

### Motion constraints
The site must also:

- remain readable
- remain performant on mobile
- support `prefers-reduced-motion`
- avoid over-animating every single component
- preserve conversion clarity

The homepage should feel like a **scroll story journey**.

### Homepage objective
The homepage should:

- show what makes Hungry different
- showcase foodies and merchants clearly
- build excitement for the app
- drive waitlist and merchant interest conversion

## 16. Conversion Strategy

### Main conversion goals
1. foodie waitlist signup
2. merchant register interest signup
3. merchant portal awareness / access

### Conversion emphasis
The site should still serve both foodies and merchants, but **merchant conversion should be slightly prioritised**.

### CTA rules
- hero must have both core CTAs
- merchant CTA should appear consistently through the site
- foodie CTA should remain strong and visible
- every major section should point naturally to the next step

### CTA language
Foodies:
- Join Waitlist
- Get Early Access

Merchants:
- Register Interest
- Put your business on Hungry
- Help customers discover your food

## 17. Technical and Implementation Direction

### Framework direction
If the site is meant to evolve into a broader web experience, the recommended foundation remains:

- **Next.js**

### Motion tooling direction
Given the highly immersive motion requirement, the implementation can consider:

- **Framer Motion** for interactive UI and transitions
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** for advanced reveal-on-scroll storytelling if necessary
- a performant carousel system for horizontal sections

### Important technical constraints
The site should remain:

- mobile-first
- fast
- SEO-friendly
- accessible
- compatible with reduced-motion settings
- maintainable in production
