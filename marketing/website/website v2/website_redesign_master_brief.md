# Hungry Website Redesign Master Brief

## 1. Executive Summary

Hungry is a Singapore food discovery app designed for two main audiences:

1. Foodies who want to discover food across Singapore
2. Merchants who want to get discovered by hungry customers

The redesigned website should be a **premium, immersive, interactive product launch website** that blends modern app storytelling with Singapore food culture.

The homepage should feel like a scroll story journey. Secondary pages can use a more traditional landing page structure with motion enhancements.
