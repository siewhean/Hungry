# CLM-04 Merchant Claim Copy Spec

## CTA Card (Stall Detail)

Headline:
- `Own this stall?`

Body:
- `Tap once to register your claim interest with our team.`

Support link:
- `See verification process`

Primary button:
- `Claim this stall`

Submitting state:
- `Submitting...`

## Toast Copy

Success:
- `Claim interest submitted. We will follow up with verification details.`

Already submitted:
- `You have already submitted a claim for this stall.`

Needs auth:
- `Please sign in before claiming this stall.`

Error:
- `Unable to submit claim right now. Please try again.`

## Verification Explainer Page

Screen title:
- `Claim Verification`

Subtitle:
- `How stall ownership verification works in Singapore`

Flow:
1. Submit claim interest from the stall page.
2. We verify business ownership via UEN or licence records.
3. If needed, we request a short video walkaround for final proof.
4. Once approved, the stall is marked verified for merchant tools.

Accepted inputs:
- UEN + registered business details.
- NEA/SFA licence details for stall operators.
- Contact name + contact email/phone for follow-up.
