## Current Scope Decision

Indexable SEO pages are limited to content with visible, useful Singapore context. Pages intentionally avoid:

- fake ratings
- fake review counts
- fake opening hours
- fake open-now status
- fake stall verification
- unsupported "best" or ranking claims

The page copy says when data is unavailable instead of filling gaps with placeholders.
